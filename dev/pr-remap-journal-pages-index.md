## Summary
Add a client setting that numbers journal pages in GM Screen cells from 1. The setting is on by default.

Foundry core numbers the compact table of contents from 0. With this setting on, the first page shows 1.

## Test
1. Put a journal with several pages into a GM Screen cell.
2. Confirm the compact table of contents starts at 1.
3. Turn the setting off and refresh the screen. Confirm it starts at 0.
